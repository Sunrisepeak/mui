# site operations

The site this page is of, asked from inside the page: what it is, its routes, the sections that answer a question, a page as it was written. The answers are the ones the `oweb` command gives of the site as built and the ones its static files hold (llms.txt, each route's index.md, site.search.xdef.json) — whichever door an agent comes by, a site says the same. Its files are fetched when first asked for, once; a reader who asks nothing pays for none.

Generated by `ops-gen` from the catalog; the effect is openas's.

| Operation | Effect | Parameters | Result | Cost | Undo | Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `site.describe` — What the site is, as it says it at each of its doors: its title, address and languages, each route with its title and its Markdown's address, the requests to go on with — and the text its build wrote as llms.txt | read | — | json | bounded | — | no |
| `routes.list` — The site's routes: each its address, source, title, language and headings with their anchors | read | — | json | bounded | — | no |
| `page.read` — One route as it was written: its Markdown (what an agent that only fetches reads) or its HTML | read | `route`: string, `as`: string (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `docs.search` — The sections of the site's pages that hold every word asked for, best first: each its route, its anchor, the headings it is under and the line that says so. The words are also put in the page's search box: who reads the page sees what was asked and what was found | read | `query`: string, `limit`: string (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |

## Tasks to begin with

Each step is a request as it is sent; `describe` gives the same to an agent.

**read-the-site** — What the site is and holds, the sections that answer a question, then a page as Markdown

```json
{"op":"site.describe"}
{"op":"docs.search","query":"install"}
{"op":"page.read","route":"/"}
```
