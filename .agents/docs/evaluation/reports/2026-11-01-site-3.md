**Benchmark summary**

[Linear](https://linear.app/) most clearly connects its proposition to recognizable workflows. [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) supplies explicit deployment, documentation and onboarding destinations. [Rive](https://rive.app/) connects live exhibits with source and authoring.

openmui’s strongest distinction is exposing the running website’s own semantics, policy and replay. Its largest gaps are visual authorship, adoption guidance and dependable transitions between representations. No overall parity with a benchmark is established.
