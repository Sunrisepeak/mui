**Defects found through use**

- **D1 — Home under side panels:** Desktop Home → Ask an agent → settle. Rendering cards retain their wide composition and disappear beneath the panel beginning around `x1060`.
- **D2 — Inaccessible desktop source:** Example 01 → horizontal wheel over source around `(1100,520)`. Long lines remain cut off; `hello/page/code` reports `max_x:0`.
- **D3 — Stale previews:** Ship Home’s checklist or edit Example 01, then enter Overview. Their textures show initial state instead of the edited state. Workshop and move-list previews substitute simplified content.
- **D4 — Playback state:** Move list → finish a move. The stage remains `animating`; `ui_wait(settled:true)` times out. Initial playback can also advance while the control still says Play.
- **D5 — Lens comprehension:** Home → Agent/Test. Repeated full paths dominate both widths; there is no node selection, filtering or linked correspondence.
- **D6 — Dry-run contradiction:** Preview `styles.show(name:"ink")`: “it cannot be undone.” Execute it, then `ui_undo`: successfully undone.
- **D7 — Texture interaction ambiguity:** In the wall, click an underlined link while its document is still a texture. The click selects the window; following requires further zoom until promotion.
- **D8 — Time-strip boundary:** Seeking an expired instant clamps to the earlier minimum, then the strip shifts; I observed a displayed offset of `−20.9 s` for the nominal 20-second window.

**Framework and openas feedback**

Stable IDs, actor journals, hit reporting, scoped undo and controllable time make the application unusually inspectable. Input is correctly attributed to `agent:codex-evaluator;via=service:input`. The scripted agent genuinely sends requests: timeline splitting and undo succeed, external opening produces `needs-answer`, and its S-10 self-answer demonstration is refused. No person’s answer was supplied.

Add truthful undo information to verb previews, scoped settling for cameras versus continuously animated content, and diagnostics for painted overflow with zero scroll range. Effective clipping should also inform semantic visibility: phone inspector controls were sometimes reported within window bounds while hidden behind the workshop viewport.

**Benchmark summary**

[Linear](https://linear.app/) most clearly connects its proposition to recognizable product workflows. [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) provides explicit deployment, documentation and technical destinations. [Rive](https://rive.app/) connects live exhibits with source, authoring and getting started.

openmui’s strongest narrow distinction is inspecting and replaying the running website’s own semantic machinery. Its largest deficits are turning that discovery into adoption, maintaining visual continuity, and completing responsive behavior. This evaluation establishes no visual, performance or accessibility parity with any benchmark.
