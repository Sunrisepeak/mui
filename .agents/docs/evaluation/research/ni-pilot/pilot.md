Keep head/neck connected through a visible trapezius and collar opening. Use asymmetry to identify Ni, rather than fabric noise. The magenta fringe, cropped bomber, exposed waist, cyan knuckle blocks/left knee pad and white soles must identify her when the picture is only 180 px high.

First hit folds the opponent at the ribs/abdomen; second hit continues upward into the chest/chin region and launches. Different compression and force direction make two contacts readable without two unrelated poses.

## Reaction library — height survives every force tier

Height means **where on the victim drawing** contact occurs: head/jaw, chest/abdomen, knee/shin/ankle. It is separate from gameplay `Level` (ordinary/low/overhead). Do not make a hit unblockable because its art touches a face. Select `(outcome, strength, anatomical region, standing/crouching/airborne, incoming direction)`; heavy never discards region.

At event onset show **R0 compression immediately**, then hold it during the victim's stop. Let `u` count unfrozen reaction frames from the contact onset; no advancement while stopped. The recovery end aligns with the actual remaining stun. Use event identity/elapsed active time, not `clip.frames - remaining_stun` to decide onset. A new hit resets to its own R0. Actor translation follows simulation pushback/launch; reaction art does not add a second gameplay displacement.
