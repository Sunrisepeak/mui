# xengine/kernel

The engine kernels below the interface (D-161): what the game layer, hanim and omui may all use. A package here depends on `mlibs`, on [xrender](../../xrender) where it draws or runs on a device, and on its neighbours here; never on [the game layer](../game), never on omui. The day the projects part, this directory is not in the game layer's repository.

| Kernel | Layer | |
|---|---|---|
| [dynamics](dynamics) | base | Every solved simulation (D-133): collision and its queries, the solver families (XPBD soft bodies; rigid bodies next), the physical materials they share; on `mlibs.simulation`'s timeline |
| [cellular](cellular) | base (its device parts gfx) | Integer rules over dense fields — transitions, flows that conserve, cells that move, a table and reactions; matter's programs lowered to it (the feature `matter`) — stepped on the CPU, sparsely where the field is at rest, or by one compute kernel ([rhi](cellular/rhi)), the same bits; a field as an agent's subject — regions, placing, time, branches, who changed a cell (the feature `agent`) — and a field of matter as one more: materials by name, why a cell is what it is back to who did what, what happened and what would (the feature `matter-agent`, D-169); a field on screen as one image, a cell's colour picked by a palette or computed by a shade with its light ([present](cellular/present)); its suite on a native device ([sdl](cellular/sdl)) |
| [matter](matter) | base | What things are made of (D-165): materials as rows of properties, each instance's state as channels, and laws that read properties and never a material's name — prepared once, stepped on any set of instances and their neighbours, the same bits everywhere, and one instance's step said aloud (`why`: each law judged, what it read, what flowed from whom); definitions as data (`*.matter.omui.ts`); what a state looks like as data too (`*.appearance.omui.ts`, D-168: colours by material and phase, states that read a channel and never a material); a carrier held to the reference step ([cts](matter/cts)); and the material lab ([lab](matter/lab), gfx): a definition, its look and a scene designed live by people and Agents — a material a file, the world played again when one changes, the agent kernel's history and claims — with the `matter-lab` command |
| [audio](audio) | media | Sounds and music as data, voices mixed into buses a block at a time with nothing allocated; a device ([device](audio/device)); a bus's gain automated by a timeline's clip ([timeline](audio/timeline)) |
| [video](video) | media | Frames and a sound track encoded on exact frame and sample counts; encoders as plugins ([webm](video/webm)) |

For lint, two projects live here (`project-dag`): the simulation kernels (dynamics, cellular, matter), which hanim may use, and the media kernels (audio, video), which may use hanim. Each kernel gives agents its state, its observation, its operations, its time, its causes and its authority from its own data model on (D-173, D-174): its README's "Agent surface" says how.
