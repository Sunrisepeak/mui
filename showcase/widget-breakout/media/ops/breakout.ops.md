# breakout operations

Play the component breakout as an agent: at the human tier as a person plays, or assisted with the field's direct actions.

Generated by `omui-gen ops` from the catalog; the effect is openas's.

| Operation | Effect | Parameters | Result | Cost | Undo | Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `field.observe` — What an agent player is shown at its tier: the step, the paddle, the balls, score, lives and bricks; assist also the rules and the hidden bricks | read | — | json | instant | — | no |
| `field.play` — A person's actions: the arrows held, Space, Z — taking effect the tier's reaction after the step it saw | reversible | `seen`: integer, `axis`: number, `launch`: bool, `look`: bool | json | frame | play again: actions hold only until the next | no |
| `field.move_to` — Moves the paddle toward x (0 … 1) at once, as a pointer does | reversible | `x`: number | — | frame | field.release gives the paddle back | no |
| `field.aim` — Holds the launch's aim until the launch | reversible | `across`: number | — | instant | the aim sweeps again after the launch | no |
| `field.launch` — Sends the ball on the paddle up, along the aim | reversible | — | — | instant | field.rewind (100 sparks) takes it back | no |
| `field.rewind` — Back that many frames for 100 sparks; playing on from there is a new branch | reversible | `frames`: integer | — | bounded | none: the sparks are spent (the branch it left is gone) | no |
| `field.next` — On from a room over | reversible | — | — | instant | field.restart plays the room again | no |

## Agent player tiers

| Tier | Shown | Reaction | Actions a second | Operations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `human` — As a person plays: what is drawn, a tenth of a second to react, ten actions a second | what is drawn | 100 ms | 10 | `field.observe`, `field.play`, `field.next` |
| `assist` — Privileged: everything shown, no delay, no limit, the field's direct actions | everything | 0 ms | no limit | `field.observe`, `field.play`, `field.move_to`, `field.aim`, `field.launch`, `field.rewind`, `field.next` |
