**Benchmark summary**

[Rive](https://rive.app/features) is strongest at connecting authoring, bones, meshes and interactive blending into one workflow. That cohesion is openmui’s largest missing capability.

GSAP leads practical choreography through [timelines](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/Timeline/), [Flip](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/Flip/) and [ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/). Basic seeking alone earns parity credit.

[Lottie](https://airbnb.tech/opensource/lottie/) leads designer interchange and portable playback. openmui has useful reactive primitives without an equivalent asset pipeline.

The clearest openmui strength is inspectable analytic motion coupled to numerical tests. No overall speed lead is established. Against the skeletal reference, [Spine’s weight editing](https://us.esotericsoftware.com/spine-weights) and [physics constraints](https://us.esotericsoftware.com/spine-physics-constraints) remain substantially more complete.
