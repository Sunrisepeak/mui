# frame operations

See which layers a frame is made of, take its pixels, and go from a pixel back to what drew it.

Generated by `omui-gen ops` from the catalog; the effect is openas's.

| Operation | Effect | Parameters | Result | Cost | Undo | Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `frame.layers` — The frame's size, its pixels' hash, and its layers from the back: each one's name, how much it drew, what it could not draw, whether it keeps what drew each pixel | read | — | json | instant | — | no |
| `frame.capture` — The pixels as drawn — the whole frame or a region of it — as a PNG in base64; at most 4 megapixels a request | read | `region`: json (optional) | json | frame | — | no |
| `frame.at` — What drew a pixel: its colour, and the chain from it back — the topmost layer that keeps what drew each pixel, and that layer's object there; of a layer in three dimensions also the instance, its mesh and material, the cluster, the triangle, the depth and the normal | read | `at`: json | json | instant | — | no |
