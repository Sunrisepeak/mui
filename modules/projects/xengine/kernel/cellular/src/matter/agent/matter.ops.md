# matter operations

Read a field of matter by material and in units, place a material, apply a quantity, ask why a cell is what it is, what happened, and what would.

Generated by `omui-gen ops` from the catalog; the effect is openas's.

| Operation | Effect | Parameters | Result | Cost | Undo | Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `matter.describe` — What the world is made of: its materials, its channels with their units, its laws by name, kind and place in the definition; one material's properties or one law in full when named | read | `material`: string (optional), `law`: string (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `matter.observe` — A region as matter: how many cells of each material, each channel in its unit (least, most, mean), how many cells are in each named state (burning) | read | `region`: json (optional), `budget`: integer (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `matter.at` — One cell: its material and phase, its channels in their units, and its properties where its state leaves them other than written (wet: a higher ignition) | read | `cell`: json | json | bounded | — | no |
| `matter.place` — Makes a region of a material anew — every channel as a new instance of it has — in one frame, recorded with who placed it | reversible | `material`: string, `region`: json | json | bounded | field.seek to the frame before it | no |
| `matter.apply` — A quantity applied to a region — a channel set to, raised by or lowered by an amount in its unit (heat: temperature add 400) — in one frame, recorded with who applied it | reversible | `channel`: string, `set`: number (optional), `add`: number (optional), `take`: number (optional), `region`: json | json | bounded | field.seek to the frame before it | no |
| `matter.explain` — Why a cell is what it is: what is on record of it, the latest first — the law that held (its name, its condition, its place in the definition), what the law read, and the neighbours' parts: who gave what it needed, who took it away, what was beside it when something ran short, each followed to its own cause and to who placed or applied what — and, for a law named, whether it holds now and which comparison fails | read | `cell`: json, `law`: string (optional), `depth`: integer (optional), `budget`: integer (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `matter.events` — What happened, oldest first: cells that became another material (by which law), named states begun and ended (ignite, go_out), edits and their actors — since a frame, of a name, in a region | read | `since`: integer (optional), `name`: string (optional), `region`: json (optional), `budget`: integer (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `matter.branch` — What would be: the field some steps ahead — a material placed or a quantity applied first when given — as the materials of a region with their change, and the events that would happen, counted by name; the field itself untouched | read | `steps`: integer (optional), `place`: json (optional), `apply`: json (optional), `region`: json (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
