# air operations

Read the air's cells and wind, apply a quantity at a place, step it, return to a frame, ask what would be, and why a cell is what it is.

Generated by `omui-gen ops` from the catalog; the effect is openas's.

| Operation | Effect | Parameters | Result | Cost | Undo | Cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `air.observe` — The frame, the state's hash, the cells and parked blocks, the strongest wind, and each channel over the field: its unit, least, most and total, and where its most is | read | `budget`: integer (optional) | json | instant | — | no |
| `air.at` — The air at a place: the cell there (none outside the cells), each channel in its unit, how warm in kelvin, and the wind | read | `at`: json | json | instant | — | no |
| `air.set` — A quantity applied to the cell at a place at the start of the next step — once, or held there every step until the next air.set of that cell and channel (a fire that keeps burning) | reversible | `at`: json, `channel`: string, `value`: number, `hold`: boolean (optional) | json | instant | air.seek to the frame before it took effect | no |
| `air.step` — Steps the air: what is applied, then the laws across the cells, then the wind moved and the heat carried | reversible | `steps`: integer (optional) | json | bounded | air.seek to the frame it began at | no |
| `air.seek` — Returns to a frame within the history kept; what is stepped from there is a new branch, and what was held is let go | reversible | `frame`: integer | json | bounded | none within the air: what came after the frame is another branch's | no |
| `air.branch` — What would be: the air some steps ahead with what is held and, if given, one more quantity applied, observed as air.observe does; the air itself untouched | read | `steps`: integer (optional), `set`: json (optional) | json | bounded | — | no |
| `air.explain` — Why the cell at a place is what it is after the last step: each law that held, what flowed in and out by which law from which neighbour (its place), what it read, and the material it became by, if it did | read | `at`: json | json | instant | — | no |

## Tasks to begin with

Each step is a request as it is sent; `describe` gives the same to an agent.

**see-the-air** — Read the field as a whole and the air at one place

```json
{"op":"air.observe"}
{"op":"air.at","at":[0.5,1.5,0.5]}
```

**light-a-fire** — Hold a cell hot, step, and read the updraft and why the cell above is warm

```json
{"op":"air.set","at":[0.5,0.5,0.5],"channel":"temperature","value":900,"hold":true}
{"op":"air.step","steps":60}
{"op":"air.at","at":[0.5,3.5,0.5]}
{"op":"air.explain","at":[0.5,1.5,0.5]}
```

**ask-before-heating** — See what a fire would do some steps ahead, the air itself untouched

```json
{"op":"air.branch","steps":60,"set":{"at":[0.5,0.5,0.5],"channel":"temperature","value":900,"hold":true}}
{"op":"air.observe"}
```
