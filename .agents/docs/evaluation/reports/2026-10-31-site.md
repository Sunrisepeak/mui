### Benchmark summary

[Linear](https://linear.app/) is strongest here in connecting a concise product promise to a coherent workflow presentation. [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) makes positioning and primary actions explicit, then offers structured technical destinations. [Rive](https://rive.app/) connects its interactive medium to concrete build, ship and learning paths.

openmui’s strongest distinction is inspectability: visitors can see semantic structure and generated test output, while code connects the scripted agent to real requests. Its largest deficits are artistic direction, first-visit storytelling and evidence of efficient browser delivery. Architectural ambition does not compensate for those deficits.
