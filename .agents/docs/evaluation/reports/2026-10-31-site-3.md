## Framework and openas feedback

Hit reporting, stable paths, semantic values and actor journals make failures unusually diagnosable. Direct `ui_act` and `ui_invoke` worked. The scripted external request returned `needs-answer`; it remained unanswered. The built-in S-10 demonstration correctly received `refused`.

The framework needs stronger nested input containment and viewport-aware embedded layouts. Semantic visibility should support the phone agent’s navigation/wait workflow. Playback controls need a shared observable clock. Tooling should distinguish stale endpoint discovery from a healthy connection and expose binary/build identity.

`ui_wait(settled:true)` is useful for animation, but does not establish that a scripted task has completed; task completion needs its own observable state.

## Benchmark summary

[Linear](https://linear.app/) is strongest in connecting positioning to recognizable product workflows. [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) makes deployment and technical destinations explicit. [Rive](https://rive.app/) connects examples with tools, runtimes, source and learning.

openmui’s narrow advantage is the page exposing its own semantic representations and reversible execution. Its largest deficits are reliable input routing, completed phone experiences and verified browser delivery quality. The semantic-tree concept does not compensate for those failures.
